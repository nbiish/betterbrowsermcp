## PREAMBLE — SOVEREIGNTY, TREATY, AND PRECEDENT DECLARATION

This license is an exercise of the inherent sovereignty of Indigenous peoples to govern
the use of their intellectual property, Traditional Knowledge (TK), and Traditional
Cultural Expressions (TCEs). It is grounded in the treaty relationship between the United
States and the Ottawa and Chippewa — including the Treaty of March 28, 1836 (7 Stat. 491),
which reserved "The Beaver islands of Lake Michigan for the use of the Beaver-island
Indians," and the Treaty of July 31, 1855 (11 Stat. 621), which named the Beaver Island
Band as a beneficiary unit — treaties that stand with federal law as the supreme law of
the land (U.S. Const. art. VI, cl. 2), and in the federal trust responsibility, UNDRIP,
and (as it enters into force) the WIPO Treaty on Intellectual Property, Genetic Resources
and Associated Traditional Knowledge.

The Rights Holder's rights of record are set out in the **Comprehensive Restricted Use
License for Indigenous Creations with Tribal Sovereignty, Data Sovereignty, and Wealth
Reclamation Protections** (the "Master License") maintained in the `license-for-all-works`
repository. Nothing in this Open Fair-Share License (the "OFSL") waives, narrows, or
estops any right in the Master License; this OFSL is an **additional tier** that grants
community freedoms the Master License does not, in exchange for fair-share contribution
once commercial success arrives.

The purpose of this OFSL is to (1) let people work on and use the Rights Holder's projects
freely in an open-source manner; (2) secure a fair share of commercial success for the
ᐙᐸᓂᒥᑮ-ᑭᓇᐙᐸᑭᓯ (Waabanimikii-Kinawaabakizi) Legacy Trust and Beaver Island Band
community programs (the "Funds"); and (3) push tribal, treaty, and federal law to new
precedent at the intersection of Indigenous intellectual property and generational wealth.
The revenue-threshold structure below presents questions of first impression; the Rights
Holder intends to develop and defend that precedent.

## 2. TIER 1 — COMMUNITY GRANT (AT OR BELOW THE FAIR-SHARE THRESHOLD)

2(a) **Grant.** While Licensee's Annual Commercial Revenue from Products is at or below
the Fair-Share Threshold, the Rights Holder grants Licensee a worldwide, royalty-free,
non-exclusive, irrevocable (while within this Tier) license to: use, copy, modify, merge,
publish, distribute, and create derivative works of the Works; and to make, use, offer,
sell, or otherwise commercialize Products — in each case for any purpose, in an
open-source manner.

2(b) **Conditions.** The Tier 1 grant is conditioned on:
   (i) **Notice retention.** Retaining the copyright notice, this license text (or a
   durable reference to it), and the attribution in Section 4 in any distribution of the
   Works or substantial portions of them;
   (ii) **No AI training.** No training, fine-tuning, or evaluation of machine-learning
   models on the Works, and no extraction of the Works for dataset construction, without
   the Rights Holder's separate Prior Informed Consent (this is a core retained right; see

   Anishinaabe or Beaver Island Band cultural heritage, TK, or TCEs; no use of the Rights
   Holder's name, likeness, or tribal identity to imply endorsement; no use touching
   Sacred Sites designations in the Master License;
   (iv) **Lawful use.** No use that violates applicable law or the rights of third
   parties.

2(c) **Measurement.** Licensee is responsible for tracking Annual Commercial Revenue from
Products. The Tier 1 grant does not require reporting at or below the Threshold, but
Licensee must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance on reasonable request.

## 3. TIER 2 — FAIR-SHARE CONTRIBUTION (ABOVE THE THRESHOLD)

3(a) **Threshold crossing.** When Licensee (with Affiliates) receives or demonstrably
expects Annual Commercial Revenue from Products exceeding the Fair-Share Threshold in a
calendar year, Licensee shall, within 30 days of crossing (or before crossing, if
reasonably anticipated), execute the Fair-Share Contribution Agreement (Exhibit A) with
the Rights Holder covering the revenue above the Threshold for that year and going
forward.

3(b) **Default terms pending agreement.** If no Fair-Share Contribution Agreement is
executed, then from the moment of crossing: (i) the Master License's benefit-sharing
terms apply to Licensee's use of the Works in full (including its minimum contribution
floor), and (ii) continued operation of Products exceeding the Threshold without an
executed agreement is an unlicensed commercial exploitation of the Works enforceable
under the Master License's enforcement architecture (Section 5 below).

3(c) **Purpose of contributions.** All contributions are paid to and administered for the
Funds: progeny education and health first, then secured income of the Rights Holder and
his family, then Beaver Island Band community programs, then reinvestment (Master License
§ 10.3A(e) priority order).

3(d) **No double payment.** Amounts paid under an executed Exhibit A are credited against
any benefit-sharing obligation for the same period under the Master License.

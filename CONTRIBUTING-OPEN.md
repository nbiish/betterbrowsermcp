## 1. PURPOSE — OPEN SOURCE MANNER, RIGHTS PRESERVED

This agreement lets anyone contribute to the Rights Holder's projects in an open-source
manner while preserving the Rights Holder's sovereign, treaty, and intellectual-property
rights and the benefit-sharing architecture that funds the ᐙᐸᓂᒥᑮ-ᑭᓇᐙᐸᑭᓯ
(Waabanimikii-Kinawaabakizi) Legacy Trust and Beaver Island Band community programs (the
"Funds"). Contributions are welcome; dilution of these rights is not.

## 2. CONTRIBUTION GRANT (INBOUND = OUTBOUND)

2(a) By submitting a contribution ("Contribution"), Contributor grants the Rights Holder
and all recipients of the project a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free,
irrevocable copyright license (and patent license for Contributor's patent claims
necessarily infringed by the Contribution as submitted) to use, reproduce, modify,
distribute, publicly perform, publicly display, and create derivative works of the
Contribution, and to sublicense these rights, under the terms of the project's license
(the OFSL's Tier 1 terms for community use; the Master License's terms for reserved
commercial and cultural uses).

2(b) Contributor's grant to the Rights Holder is at least as broad as the grant Contributor
receives for using the project; nothing in this agreement grants Contributor any rights in
the Rights Holder's name, tribal identity, marks, TK, or TCEs.

## 3. CONTRIBUTOR REPRESENTATIONS

Contributor represents that: (a) the Contribution is Contributor's original work or
Contributor has the right to submit it under these terms; (b) the Contribution does not
infringe any third party's rights; (c) Contributor has the authority to make the grant in
Section 2; and (d) Contributor will notify the Rights Holder of any third-party materials
included and their license terms.

## 4. CULTURAL HERITAGE GUARDRAILS

4(a) Contributions must not contain, depict, or derive from Anishinaabe or Beaver Island
Band Traditional Knowledge, Traditional Cultural Expressions, ceremonial content, sacred
sites material, or cultural heritage unless Contributor is authorized by the Rights Holder
(or the competent cultural authority) to submit it. Unauthorized cultural material will be
rejected and removed.

4(b) The Rights Holder may reject, modify the scope of, or request changes to any
Contribution that conflicts with the cultural protections, benefit-sharing architecture,
or sovereign rights preserved by the Master License — including the Funds priority order
(Master License § 10.3A(e)) — and Contributor will reasonably cooperate.

## 5. ATTRIBUTION AND RECORDS

Contributions are credited in the project's contributor records (e.g., commit history,
NOTICE file, or contributors list). Contributor consents to that credit and to the
retention of contribution records for provenance and audit. No other publicity uses of
Contributor's name are granted.

## 6. PRESERVATION OF BENEFIT-SHARING

Contributor acknowledges that the Works are licensed under a revenue-threshold fair-share
structure: community use is free below the Fair-Share Threshold, and commercial success
above it contributes to the Funds (OFSL § 3). A Contribution does not entitle Contributor
to any share of the Funds or of other users' contributions, and does not alter the
threshold or rates.

## 7. SIGN-OFF (DCO STYLE)

Each Contribution should carry a sign-off line certifying Section 3:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

using a real name or a documented pseudonym. The sign-off is the record of this
agreement; no separate CLA paperwork is required unless the Rights Holder requests it for
a specific contribution.

## 8. DISPUTES

Sections 5 and 8 of the OFSL and Master License §§ 11–13 (including § 12.7
automatic-trigger enforcement) govern: tribal law and federal Indian law; tribal court,
federal court, or arbitration at the Rights Holder's election; ambiguities resolved in
favor of sovereignty.
